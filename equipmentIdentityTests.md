# Equipment identity smoke checks

- Physical equipment instances have distinct IDs and authored design names.
- Royal Guard, City Watch, goblin and orc shields use canonical presets.
- Shield heraldry belongs to the equipped item instance, not the character/base item ID.
- Plain goblin/orc shields retain unpainted art.
- Free appearance controls are dev/design-service only.
- Crafted and commissioned helpers preserve chosen appearance and provenance.
